# Pith Imp
### Where does the stolen spell go?
It goes to a separate zone as if the Pith Imp was carrying the spell; _it does not go to your hand_. You may look at the spell and your opponent may not (though they do get to see the spell at the moment you steal it).
### If Pith Imp somehow gains Spellcaster, can it pretty please cast the spell it stole?
Yes, that does seem fun!
