# Mountain Giant
### What happens if a Mountain Giant occupies a Silence aura?
A card's size is generally set when it enters the realm, and is considered one of its characteristics, not an ability. It will not resize.
### If Polar Explorers or Ruler of Thul are in play and an oversized unit on the top row moves “up” one row to wrap around to the bottom, what happens?
This ability only allows movement across the borders, not ongoing occupation. Units cannot balance on the edge of the realm and an oversized unit that finds its way there falls to one side or the other. Its controller places it in the closest legal position, controller's choice if tied.
### If Magellan Globe is in play and an Oversized unit on the top row moves “up” one row to wrap around to the bottom, what happens?
Since Magellan Globe is an ongoing effect that connects the top and bottom edges, the oversized unit can occupy 2 locations on the top row and 2 locations on the bottom row. Ensure that game state is clear, using notes as needed.
### If Magellan Globe leaves play when a card is spanning the edge of the realm, what happens?
Units cannot balance on the edge of the realm and an oversized unit that finds its way there falls to one side or the other. Its controller places it in the closest legal position, controller's choice if tied.

If there's no legal position anywhere in the realm for a minion, it dies. If there's no legal position anywhere in the realm for an avatar, shrink it until there is a legal position (Avatar controller's choice), and place it there.
