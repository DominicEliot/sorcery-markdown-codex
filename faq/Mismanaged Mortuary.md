# Mismanaged Mortuary
### Once this is in play, do I put cards into the cemetery that I own or the cemetery that I control?
You always put your cards into the cemetery you own (the one ))nearby(( your deck).
### If I have Wormelow Tump in play, what happens?
The cemeteries are still swapped, and you protect everything in "your" cemetery (which is really your opponent's).
### If there are an even number of Mismanaged Mortuaries in play, do they cancel out?
Yes.
