# Lure
### Can my allied minion tempt an enemy in a different region to take a step closer?
Yes, as long as they occupy a nearby site, they can be above or below that site.  However, they cannot be tempted to take a step into a region they couldn't normally move into (e.g. they cannot be made to step underground if they do not have Burrowing).
### If the tempted enemy has multiple equally closer steps, who chooses the specific step taken?
The caster of this spell determines among the equally closer choices.
