# The Hexham Haunts
### How does summoning burrowed interact with Evil Presence?
You may summon the Haunts to an affected site, and they must be burrowed. But the Haunts won't gain Charge and Evil Presence will remain in the realm because the Haunts were not summoned "here" according to Evil Presence, since Evil Presence occupies the surface (and perhaps the void), not underground.
