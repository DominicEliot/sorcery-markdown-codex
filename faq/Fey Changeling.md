# Fey Changeling
### Can this be summoned to an inhospitable location (e.g. underground) and die, but still have a chance to use its genesis ability first?
No. You cannot choose to summon a minion underground, underwater, or in the void unless it has the appropriate ability.
### The term "summon" means "enter the realm." So, does that mean I can play this minion for free?
No. This effect simply provides additional locations where you may summon this minion.
