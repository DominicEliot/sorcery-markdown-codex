# Seirawan Hydra
### How can I kill a Hydra?
Deal 1 lethal damage, or 6 damage all at once. Multiple sources of simultaneous damage will work. For example, if the Hydra attacks and two [[Bosk Troll]]s defend, the Hydra will be killed.

Note that damage done one at a time, e.g. [[Arcane Barrage]], would not kill it, since each individual 1 damage would be immediately healed.
