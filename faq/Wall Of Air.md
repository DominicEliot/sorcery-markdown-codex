# Wall Of Air
### Does this affect subsurface units?
No. (They are not atop the site.)
### What if this is animated by an Enchantress?
An animated Wall of Air can’t move (unless it gains power), since it has a Wall of Air in its middle! If it gains power (or teleports) and manages to move, the Wall of Air applies between the two squares it occupies.
### Does this affect the corners of the border? (e.g. Airborne units moving diagonally)
No. "Border" means adjacent squares, not including diagonals.
### How many squares does this aura occupy?
2
