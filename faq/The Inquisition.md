# The Inquisition
### What happens if my opponent searches my spellbook but doesn't see this card when searching?
If it's in your spellbook and they could see it, The Inquisition will trigger and be summoned! Tell your opponent that the card is in your spellbook and reveal it to them.

If your opponent is only searching a limited part of your spellbook (e.g. Haystack), The Inquisition will only trigger if it was in the part of your spellbook that was actually searched. If there's a question, a judge can verify that The Inquisition wasn't there.
### When does this ability trigger?
If an effect requires you to reveal it from your hand, or from your spellbook, or if your opponent searches your spellbook.
### What happens if I name this card when playing Feast for Crows?
You would complete the search effect and banish The Inquisition before it triggers. Turns out sometimes you _do _expect them.
