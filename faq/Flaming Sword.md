# Flaming Sword
### If the attack damage was increased or decreased, does the splash damage include the change?
Yes. For example, Critical Strike will splash double damage.
### If a unit has two Flaming Swords, do they each splash damage?
Yes. Have fun dual wielding!
### Is the splash damage a strike?
No. It’s damage done by the sword artifact.
### Does the splash damage occur whenever the unit strikes?
Yes. For example, that includes Grapple Shot or other strikes, not just attacks from the Move and Attack basic ability.
### If the struck unit isn’t an enemy, is any damage splashed?
No. If there's no "struck unit," then it has no location. Also, there are no “other enemies” when there’s no enemy initially. See "((enemy))" in the codex.
### If the Battlemage with a Flaming Sword attacks one enemy, and then another enemy dies as a result of the Flaming Sword, does the Battlemage get to draw a card?
Yes. An attack was made, and then a minion was killed. As long as your attacking unit used the Move and Attack ability and then got kill credit, this ability triggers.
