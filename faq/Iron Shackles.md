# Iron Shackles
### Can I target a burrowed minion or a voidwalk minion?
Not unless you have a burrowed spellcaster or a spellcaster in the void. Since the effect uses the word "target," the spellcaster must be in the same region as the target. (There are four regions: surface, underground, underwater, void.)
### What happens if I target a minion with a Ward when conjuring this?
On resolution, the Ward will protect the targeted minion. Due to the Golden Rule, the Ward breaks and the Shackles end up at the same location, not carried.
