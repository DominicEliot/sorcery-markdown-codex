# Sparkmage
### Can any of my spellcasters cast a spell and still benefit from my avatar's ability?
Yes! As long as you control the spellcaster, the game sees it as a spell "((you))" cast, meaning the player.
### Is the damage considered air damage?
Yes.
### If I give my Sparkmage a poisonous dagger, will it deal lethal damage with its tap ability?
Yes, because it's dealing the damage directly via it's ability. (Damage from a lightning bolt cast by the Sparkmage would not be lethal.)
### If I reduce the threshold required to cast the spell (e.g. Mix Aer), does the threshold still count for the Sparkmage ability?
Yes. The Sparkmage ability considers printed threshold on the spell.
