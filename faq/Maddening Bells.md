# Maddening Bells
### If Maddening Bells tries to increase the cost of a spell that has an alternate casting cost of zero (e.g. Deathspeaker ability while on death's door, Aramos Mercenaries), what happens?
The spell costs zero. See "((Cost))" for more details.
### When summoning minions, does Maddening Bells care about the location where the minion is summoned, or where the spellcaster is?
Only where the spellcaster is, not the summoning location.
### If the Spellcaster is nearby Maddening Bells but the spell affects something not nearby, does it still cost 2 extra mana?
Yes. Maddening Bells’s ability only cares about the Spellcaster’s position.
