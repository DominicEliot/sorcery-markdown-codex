# Scatter
### Can I push an oversized unit?
Yes, choose a cardinal direction and push the oversized unit one step in that direction as long as no part of the unit would change regions.
### Does the site itself get pushed?
No.
### Can I choose different directions for each card being pushed?
Yes.
### Can I push something into another region?
No, "pushing" is a form of forced movement which cannot cause something to change regions unless it specifically says so.
