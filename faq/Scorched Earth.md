# Scorched Earth
### A minion with Gilded Aegis is on a site chosen for Scorched Earth. The Aegis and the minion are being destroyed simultaneously. Does the Aegis have a chance to save the minion before it’s destroyed?
Yes.
### If I target Bedrock with Scorched Earth, the Bedrock obviously isn’t destroyed, but is everything else still destroyed?
Yes. (Remember Avatars cannot be destroyed.)
### Can I choose zero sites?
Yes. “Any number” includes zero.
