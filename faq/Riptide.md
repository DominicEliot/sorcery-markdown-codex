# Riptide
### Can riptide pull a voidwalk minion in the void?
No, because minions in the void aren’t “aboveground.”
### What are the details of pulling an oversized minion that occupies the water site and other locations?
When pulling or pushing a oversized unit, choose a single location the oversized unit occupies (and meets the requirement of the effect - e.g. adjacent), and push/pull from there.
