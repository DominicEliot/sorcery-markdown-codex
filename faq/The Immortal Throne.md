# The Immortal Throne
### What does "alone here" mean?
No other units at the location.
### What counts as "playing" a card?
Playing a site, which costs 0, or casting a spell.
### If I modify the cost of a spell (e.g. a Mix artifact, or an alternate casting cost), does The Immortal Throne look at the modified cost or the printed cost?
Cost in this case means "printed cost."
### The alpha foil version has the text for Pendulum of Peril on it. Is that a misprint?
Yes. Use the text for the normal version. The foil version is not legal for tournament ))play((.
