# Demonic Contract
### What is all of the small text on this card?!
Don't tell me you entered into a contract with a Demon without reading the fine print! The smaller text is all of the rules and stipulations for the casting of this spell!
### What is "lower" rarity?
The order of rarities (from highest to lowest) is: Unique, Elite, Exceptional, and Ordinary. For example, if you control an Exceptional Demon, you could search for an Exceptional or Ordinary spell.
