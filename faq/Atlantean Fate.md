# Atlantean Fate
### If one of the sites it occupies is an Ordinary water site, are minions and artifacts there still submerged?
Yes. It’s still affected by the aura, but that effect doesn’t do anything to Ordinary sites, other than the Genesis ability.
### Can you play Rift Valley into an area affected by Atlantean Fate?
Yes, since Atlantean Fate only affects the site once it’s already in play, and Rift Valley modifies how it’s played in the first place.
### If my Vindictive Nation, or an allied site near it, is affected by my opponent's Atlantean Fate, how much life do they lose?
One life. Atlantean Fate will flood and silence Vindictive Nation, as well as prevent it from providing non-water threshold. These are all modifications to the site, all from a single effect on the storyline. Since these all come from a single effect on the storyline, Vindictive Nation sees it as a single modification.
