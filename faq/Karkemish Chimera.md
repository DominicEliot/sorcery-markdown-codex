# Karkemish Chimera
### Units A, B, and C are being attacked; can unit D defend the attack and protect A, B, and C, or just one of them?
Just one; you must choose unit A, B, or C to be defended.
### Units A, B, and C are being attacked; can unit A defend the attack against B, so that B takes no damage?
No, since A is already being attacked, they cannot take the Defend reaction.
### Do each of the attacked units take 6 damage, or is the damage split as the attacker chooses?
The damage is split as the attacker chooses.
