# Displace
### Can this teleport a minion or artifact from a site to the void?
No. The location the minion or artifact teleports to must be one diagonal step away from where it started, and steps are only measured within the same region. A minion or artifact starting at a surface location can only be teleported to another surface location, for example.
### Can this teleport an aura from a site to the void?
Yes! When moving auras, you ignore the regional boundaries between surface and void locations.
