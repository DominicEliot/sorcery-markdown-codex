# Vaults Of Zul
### If I play this under my avatar, have I stopped there?
No, because a unit doesn't enter the site if the unit was already present in the square when the site was played.
### What does "stops here" mean?
An avatar stops when there is no additional movement for that unit on the Storyline. A unit must first move to then stop.
### Does Vaults of Zul trigger once per game, or once per game per avatar?
Once per game total. They took time to loot the vaults! (If two avatars somehow stop on the vaults simultaneously, they will both be tied for first, and will both loot the vaults.)
### What happens if my turn is skipped via Vaults of Zul and Courtesan Thaïs is affecting me?
If a turn is skipped then there's no actual turn, which means that part of Courtesan Thaïs's effect would wait for that player's next actual turn.
