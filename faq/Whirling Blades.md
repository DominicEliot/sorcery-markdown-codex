# Whirling Blades
### Does the path include the starting and ending locations?
Yes.
### Can a unit with move-related abilities (e.g. Airborne) “take steps” using its ability?
Yes.
### Do I fully resolve my movement first, then strike each enemy simultaneously?
Yes.
### If I choose to move 0 steps (perhaps because my unit is Immobile), do I strike anything?
No, because you don't have any path if you don't move. A path involves 1 or more steps, and each step has a starting and ending location that's different (if your step is from a location to the same location, you didn't actually move anywhere.)
### Can I choose to move a step forward and then back to strike an enemy twice with this?
No. You can choose to step forward and back, but an enemy is either along your path or not. They will not be struck more than once.
