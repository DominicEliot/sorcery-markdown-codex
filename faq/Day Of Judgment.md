# Day Of Judgment
### Can I rotate the cross?
Yes.
### Must the cross be entirely within the realm?
Yes. Evil can cower safely in the corners!
### Does this affect multiple regions, such as the void and subsurface as well?  

Yes! The card text uses "affected squares," which include surface, subsurface, and void.
### How do I properly resolve the banish and damage effects?
Banish Evil minions first, resolving effects (if any) as a result of their banishment. Then, damage all units occupying those six squares.
