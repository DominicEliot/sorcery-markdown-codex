# Lord Of Fear
### What does "defend alone" mean?
When the Defend window closes (see "((Attack))" in the codex for details), at least two units must be in the fight. If not, any unit that used the Defend ability is removed from the fight and the original attack target is reinstated. Defenders still arrive one at a time; if there isn’t at least two units after the Defend window, the defense is effectively negated (though that one defender still got to tap and move to the attack's location).
