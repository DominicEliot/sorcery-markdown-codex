# Common Cottagers
### Are these family cards of Castle Servants, Common Cottagers, Blacksmith Family, and Fisherman's Family considered Air, Earth, Fire, and Water spells, respectively? 
Yes. You can see the spell’s colored frame matches those elements, so Castle Servants is an Air spell, Common Cottagers is an Earth spell, Blacksmith Family is a Fire spell, and Fisherman’s Family is a Water Spell.
