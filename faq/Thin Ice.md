# Thin Ice
### How does the resolution of this ability interact with other “end of turn” triggers?
The ability is placed on the storyline with all other end of turn triggers. Then, when it resolves, if the total power of minions on the surface of the site is 5 or greater, submerge them.

For example, if you control a [[Questing Beast]] atop your Thin Ice, you could order the end of turn triggers so that the Beast takes a step away from the Thin Ice first, then you would only submerge other minions atop Thin Ice if their total power was still 5 or greater (not counting the Questing Beast).
