# Land Deed
### What happens if I have a minion that occupies more than one site (e.g. Mountain Giant) and also carries Land Deed?
Land Deed only grants you control of the site that Land Deed occupies.
### If I gain control of a site in the middle of my turn, does it produce mana immediately?
Yes, if you gain control of a site during your turn, you may immediately use the mana it provides.
### What happens if I have a Land Deed on Rubble?
You control the Rubble and your avatar will lose life if the Rubble takes damage, but the Rubble still doesn’t provide mana. i.e. you own a hunk of worthless rock!
