# Golden Dawn
### How do I resolve this effect?
Deal face up to your back row one at a time, placing each card to the surface of a site as you go. Then, shuffle any non-minions and put them on the bottom of your deck. Now all the minions are summoned simultaneously to the location they were dealt to; resolve any effects of those minions being summoned, e.g. genesis effects. Finally, check to see if you're at Death's Door now, and if so, all the summoned minions gain Charge.
