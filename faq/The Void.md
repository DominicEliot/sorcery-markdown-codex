# The Void
### How does this site work?
The site still has both a surface and subsurface, but those locations also count as void simultaneously. It has a surface + void location, and a subsurface + void location.

Minions without voidwalk that enter the site will be banished.

Spells cast from the surface + void location can target cards that are in the surface or the void, for example.

Projectiles can travel from the surface + void location into the void.
### Can my avatar enter this site?
Yes.
### Can forced movement move a minion from a surface location to the surface of The Void?
Yes!
### When my opponent casts a minion spell with Voidwalk, can they choose The Void as its summoning location, even though it's my site?
Yes! It is a void, so they can choose to cast their Voidwalk minions to here if they wish.
### While my Avatar occupies the surface + void location of The Void site, can I target something occupying the underground + void location of The Void site?
Yes! The target is in the same region (void) as the spellcaster, so it can be targeted!
### Normally, when moving from void to a site, a minion (e.g. Hounds of Ondaros) can move directly to the surface OR subsurface. Therefore, when moving from the **subsurface + void location** of this site, can a minion like Hounds of Ondaros move directly to the surface of an adjacent site? Could they move from the **surface + void** location directly to the subsurface of an adjacent site?
Yes! Minions with voidwalk can continue to use their voidwalk abilities to move accordingly.
