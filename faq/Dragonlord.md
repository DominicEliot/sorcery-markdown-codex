# Dragonlord
### When do I choose my set aside Dragon? Can it change during a tournament?
You must select your set aside Dragon "before setup," which means during deckbuilding. You cannot change it during a tournament. The Dragonlord is loyal to his champion and would not be so fickle!
### Can my spellbook include a copy of my set aside Unique Dragon?
Yes! The set aside Dragon is intended as a rules reference for both players. You may use a suitable substitute for the set aside Dragon, if needed.
### When the Dragonlord gains the abilities of the set aside Dragon, does the Dragonlord gain its power as well?
No, power is a characteristic, not an ability. The Dragonlord will only gain the abilities printed in the text box.
### Can the Dragonlord gain Stealth?
No. Avatars cannot gain Stealth.
### If the Dragonlord gains Voidwalk, Burrowing, or Submerge, may it enter other regions?
No. Avatars cannot enter any region other than the surface.
### If the Dragonlord gains Ignis Rex's ability, is that considered fire damage? Same question for Adtonitum and air damage?
Yes! If the Dragonlord gains Ignis Rex's ability and then discards a fire card, the Dragonlord will deal fire damage. Same answer for Adtonitum and air damage.
