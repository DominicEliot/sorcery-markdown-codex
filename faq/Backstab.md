# Backstab
### Can I target my opponent’s minion(s) with Backstab?
Yes (but remember the spellcaster must be in the same region as all the targets).
### If a minion has voidwalk and is in the void, could Backstab move it to an adjacent site to strike a tapped minion there?
No. Your spellcaster must target both minions (which are in different regions), but that's impossible since you pick a single location the spellcaster occupies for casting a spell. You can only target minions in the region where the spell is being cast. See "((Casting Spells))" for more details.
